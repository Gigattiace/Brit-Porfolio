02 / About This Project

My Digital Universe

A personal catalog inspired by the things I enjoy.

Anime & Manga

This section collects some of my favorite anime, manga, and manhwa titles. It includes series such as K-On!, Yuru Camp, Grand Blue, and Kaoru Hana wa Rin to Saku.

Gaming

The gaming section includes titles I enjoy or want to feature, including Minecraft, VALORANT, Genshin Impact, Roblox, Blood Strike, and Elden Ring.

Favorites

Use the heart button on a card to save a title. Favorites are stored in your browser so they can remain available after you close or refresh the page.

Technologies

This project uses HTML for structure, CSS for the visual design, and JavaScript for searching, filtering, the details popup, and saved favorites.
